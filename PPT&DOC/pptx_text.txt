IDEA / SOLUTION
A centralized system that predicts land-acquisition delay risks before they become critical, enabling authorities to take preventive action.
OUR IDEA:
LandGuard
 AI
 predicts land-acquisition delay risks at every project stage. 
It analyzes 
revenue, legal, R&R, and project data
 to identify risks, explains their causes, and 
alerts officials for early action
.
Predict → Explain → Alert → Act
INNOVATION & UNIQUENESS
❖ 
Stage-wise Intelligence
 — Predicts risk at individual acquisition stages rather than only overall project delay
❖ 
Explainable Predictions
 — Shows 
why
 a case is at risk instead of producing a black-box score
❖ 
Bottleneck Intelligence
 — Detects recurring issues affecting multiple projects, regions or departments
❖ 
Proactive Decision Support
 — Moves beyond monitoring by identifying risks early and supporting preventive action
❖ 
Predict → Explain → Act
 — Connects AI prediction directly with administrative intervention
PROBLEM:
Land acquisition involves 
multiple departments, complex procedures, and fragmented records
, making delays difficult to detect early. This leads to 
cost overruns, disputes, and stalled projects
.
❖ 
Predictive Intelligence
 – Stage-wise delay prediction using revenue, legal, R&R & project data.
❖ 
Actionable Monitoring
 – GIS dashboard highlights high-risk projects with automated alerts & recommendations.
❖ 
Smart Document & Risk Analysis
 – NLP/OCR processes legacy records while Explainable AI identifies key risk factors.

TECHNICAL APPROACH
3
SOFTWARE / TECHNOLOGIES
❖ 
Python (Pandas, NumPy)
 – Handles data cleaning, transformation, 
preprocessing
 and feature engineering for land-acquisition records.
❖ 
Database
 – Maintains structured project, land, legal, compensation and R&R records for centralized analysis.
❖ 
OCR + NLP
 – Extracts information from scanned and legacy documents using OCR, 
spaCy
 and Legal-BERT for legal-text analysis.
❖ 
XGBoost
 / 
LightGBM
 – Uses historical and current acquisition data to predict delay risks at individual project stages.
❖ 
SHAP / LIME
 – Provides explainable predictions by identifying the major factors contributing to each delay-risk score.
❖ 
Flask/Django + React.js + GIS
 – Integrates backend APIs, interactive dashboards, geographic risk visualization and automated alerts.

FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
4
FEASIBILITY:
❖ 
Technical
 – Proven ML/NLP models can integrate through standard REST APIs.
❖ 
Operational
 – Fits existing district revenue workflows without disrupting current procedures.
❖ 
Data
 – OCR and NLP enable processing of both structured and legacy records.
❖ 
Cost
 – Open-source technologies keep development and deployment costs low.
VIABILITY:
❖ 
Scalable
 – Can expand from pilot districts to state-level implementation.
❖ 
Integrable
 – Designed to work alongside existing land-record systems and portals.
❖ 
Actionable
 – Risk scores, explanations and alerts directly support officials' decisions.
❖ 
Long-term
 – Continuous feedback improves predictions as more acquisition cases are processed.
CHALLENGES:
❖ 
Data Fragmentation
 – Revenue, legal and R&R records remain distributed across departments.
❖ 
Legacy Documents
 – Records may be scanned, inconsistent or in regional languages.
❖ 
Data Quality
 – Missing or outdated records can affect prediction accuracy.
❖ 
User Trust
 – Officials may hesitate to rely on unexplained AI predictions.
MITIGATION STRATEGY
:
❖ 
Unified ETL + OCR
 – Standardizes data from multiple departments and legacy documents.
❖ 
Multilingual NLP
 – Extracts useful information from regional-language records.
❖ 
Explainable AI
 – SHAP/LIME shows the factors behind each risk score.
❖ 
Human-in-the-Loop
 – Officer feedback and case outcomes continuously improve the system.
